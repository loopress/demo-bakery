Our story

Two bakers, one old oven

We opened on Rue des Tanneurs in 2023, in a shop that had been a bakery for a hundred years before us.

Slow on purpose

Most of what we bake starts the day before. The sourdough ferments overnight, the croissant dough rests between each fold, and the brioche gets a full night in the cold.

It takes longer, and it is the whole point: time does more for flavour than any improver ever will.

Local flour, real butter

Our wheat and rye come from a mill forty kilometres away. The butter is AOP, the eggs are free range, and the fruit on the tarts changes with the market.
